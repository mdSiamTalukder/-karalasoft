Homepage design directions

Four independent homepage builds for KaralaSoft. All take their typographic system, scale discipline and layout rhythm from Netguru — but none of them copies it. Each keeps your emerald brand and your real content. Pick a direction and I'll develop it into the live site.

DM Sans / DM Mono Netguru-derived scale Zero JS dependencies Responsive Reduced-motion safe Distinct art direction each

What's shared, what isn't

Shared across all of them: Netguru's type scale (6rem/1.1 display, 3rem/1.25 section heads), its 0.6rem/1.2rem radii, cubic-bezier(.33,1,.68,1) easing, scroll-reveal with a safety sweep, and count-up stats. Deliberately different: palette treatment, hero composition, section rhythm, and how services and work are presented.

Direction B

Brand Poster

Inverted and loud. Full-bleed emerald hero with near-black type, outlined display word, 2px borders and offset shadows throughout. Services become a numbered table; ticker bands divide the sections. Highest memorability, and the one that most needs a designer to restrain it.
